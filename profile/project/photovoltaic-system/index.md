## Project overview

This master’s research project at the University of Lubumbashi focused on the design and realization of an optimized photovoltaic system, including system design and performance analysis.

It forms part of my earlier work in electrical engineering and energy systems, before my transition toward mathematical sciences and computational plasma physics.
