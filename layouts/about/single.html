A path shaped

From electrical engineering to mathematical sciences and plasma physics, my work connects physical questions with computational tools.

I am a PhD researcher in Nuclear Engineering and Radiological Sciences at the University of Michigan, working with Prof. Peng Zhang in the Plasma, Beams, and Interface Science Group.

My academic path has taken me from the University of Lubumbashi to AIMS Rwanda, Michigan State University, and the University of Michigan. Alongside research, I have taught engineering and physics, supervised students, and volunteered in programming and robotics education.

Outside research, I enjoy history and film. I speak English, French, and Swahili.
